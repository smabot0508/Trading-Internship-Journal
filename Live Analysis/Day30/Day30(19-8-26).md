## Key Takeaways From Day 30

1. **The 9 EMA/VWAP crossover concept now has a 10-3 record across three sessions (Days 28–30)**, a genuinely useful sample size suggesting real, repeatable edge — particularly notable given today's trades spanned both directions (2 longs, 1 short passing cleanly, plus 2 more longs) and multiple unrelated stocks.

3. **HPE's trade is a good reminder that "target hit" and "the stock kept going the trader's way afterward" are separate outcomes worth tracking distinctly** — the short hit target on the decline, then the stock reversed hard and rallied; the trade's own result was clean and unaffected by what happened next.
4. **A clean 4-for-4 day, the first since Day 20, is worth logging without over-crediting** — as flagged after Day 20's own streak, perfect sessions are a good moment for continued discipline rather than complacency, especially with a newer setup (the crossover) still building its track record.

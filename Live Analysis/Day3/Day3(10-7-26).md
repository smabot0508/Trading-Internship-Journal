### 🟢 SNDK — LONG — ✅ **PASSED**

**Setup: Liquidity Sweep below Previous Day Low, reversal toward Previous Day High**

Price opened the session, then wicked sharply below the **Previous Day Low (1,801.14)** down toward ~1,780 — sweeping the resting sell-side liquidity that had built up under that level. This is a classic liquidity grab: the market pushes just past an obvious prior level to trigger stops before reversing.

Immediately after the sweep, a strong displacement candle reversed price back up through 1,800 and into an extended rally zone (marked in green on the chart), which carried price all the way up to retest the **Previous Day High (1,951.74)**.

| Parameter | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | **~1,808–1,820** | Reclaim above the swept Previous Day Low, confirming the sweep was rejected |
| Stop Loss | **~1,775** | A few points below the sweep wick low |
| Target | **1,951.74** | Previous Day High — the objective the whole move was built around |
| **Result** | **✅ Passed** — price rallied into the 1,940–1,951 zone, tagging the Previous Day High target, before pulling back to close around 1,916–1,920 | Clean liquidity-sweep-and-reversal trade, textbook execution |

### 🟢 NVDA — LONG — ✅ **PASSED**

**Setup: Bullish Engulfing Candlestick + Confirmation**

After a small pullback into the ~202.78–204.76 zone, a **bullish engulfing candle** printed — a candle that fully engulfs the prior red candle's range to the downside, signaling a sharp shift from selling to buying pressure. This was followed by a **confirmation candle** (a second green candle continuing in the same direction), which is what actually triggers the entry rather than the engulfing candle alone.

| Parameter | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | **~205** | On the close of the confirmation candle following the engulfing pattern |
| Stop Loss | **~202.78** | Below the low of the engulfing candle / recent support |
| Target | **211** | Measured target zone (top of the marked green box) |
| **Result** | **✅ Passed** — price climbed step-by-step from the entry all the way to a high of **211.10**, tagging the target almost exactly | One of the cleanest setups of the week — every step (pullback, engulfing candle, confirmation, follow-through) lined up |

**Chart:**

![NVDA Trade — bullish engulfing candle plus confirmation, target hit at 211](Images/NVDA-Trade-Day3.jpeg)

**Why it worked:** The key lesson here is the **confirmation candle requirement** — entering directly on the engulfing candle (before it closes, or without waiting for the next candle) is a lower-quality entry than waiting for a second candle to confirm the reversal is holding. This setup respected that rule and paid off with a clean, uninterrupted move to target.

### 🟢 MU — TRADE 2: LONG — ✅ **PASSED**

**Setup: FVG + Confirmation in Higher Timeframe (reversal off the Day Low)**

Once price hit the Day Low, it reversed and rallied back up, **mitigating FVG 1 and FVG 2 on the way up** (filling in the imbalance left behind by Trade 1's drop). The key entry signal was where price broke back above the FVG 2 zone with a confirmation candle on a **higher timeframe** — labeled directly on the chart as "FVG + Confirmation in Higher Timeframe." This is a stronger signal than a lower-timeframe confirmation alone, since the reversal is showing up consistently across timeframes, not just on the 5-minute chart.

| Parameter | Level | Notes |

| Entry | **~982–984** | On the higher-timeframe confirmation candle closing back above the FVG 2 zone |
| Stop Loss | **~957** | Below the Day Low sweep — if this breaks, the reversal thesis is invalidated |

## Key Takeaways From Day 3

1. **SNDK** was the cleanest example this week of the core Liquidity Sweep concept from Day 8: a sweep below an obvious level (Previous Day Low), followed by an uninterrupted move to the next obvious level (Previous Day High) once that liquidity was absorbed.
2. **NVDA** reinforced why a confirmation candle matters — the engulfing candle alone was the signal, but waiting for the next candle to confirm it before entering is what made this a high-quality, low-drama trade to target.
3. **MU delivered two separate, fully valid trades on one chart:** the liquidity sweep above Day High wasn't just a warning sign to sit out — it was a tradeable short in its own right, targeting the Day Low. Then, once price reached the Day Low and showed genuine reversal signs (FVG mitigation + higher-timeframe confirmation), that was a second, independent long trade. The lesson isn't "wait for the better setup and ignore the first one" — it's "recognize when a chart is offering two distinct, sequential setups and be ready to trade both."
4. **Process note:** all four trades today were built around the same underlying idea — a sweep of resting liquidity followed by confirmation before entering — just expressed differently on each chart (reclaim of a swept level, engulfing + confirmation candle, and FVG mitigation + higher-timeframe confirmation). Recognizing that these are variations of the same core concept is more useful long-term than treating each one as a separate pattern.

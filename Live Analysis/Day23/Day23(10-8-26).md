## Key Takeaways From Day 23

1. **TEAM is this journal's cleanest example yet of a genuine structural earnings turn** — not just a beat, but a company's *first positive GAAP operating margin in over two years*, paired with RPO growth (44%) meaningfully outpacing headline guidance (13%). When a beat comes with real margin-structure improvement, not just a one-quarter revenue pop, it's worth weighting more heavily than a standard beat.
2. **PLTR's full round-trip is an important nuance to internalize clearly: a trade "passing" means the target was reached, not that the stock's subsequent price action was predicted or even desirable to watch happen.** The position would have been closed at 179.02 regardless of what came after — conflating "the stock round-tripped" with "the trade failed" would be a mistake going forward.
3. **HPE repeats the exact lesson from Day 21's MDB: a directionally correct short can still get stopped out if the range takes longer to resolve than the stop allows for.** Both trades were vindicated by the eventual price action — just too late to matter for the position actually taken. Worth considering wider stops (or smaller size with a wider stop) specifically for range-fade setups with no catalyst driving the timing.
4. **The new "CANSLIM 2" merged screener produced a genuinely different, higher-quality candidate list on its first day out** — three of four trades were repeat names from the CANSLIM Screener era, suggesting the extra pre-market-gap filter is refining rather than replacing what was already working.
5. **Next Pre-Market session:** watch whether NET and TEAM can extend their moves a further session, and whether PLTR's round-trip today was a one-off or the start of the exhaustion this journal has been watching for since Day 19.

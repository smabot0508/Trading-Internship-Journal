1. **DELL was both the best and a losing trade in the same session** — the morning long (+$2.91) rode real strength, but shorting it later at a much higher price (-$0.95) fought that same strength. The lesson from Day 5/Day 8 about not fading a stock that's clearly trending stands out again here.
2. **MU was a genuinely good addition to the rotation** — both MU trades were profitable, and the short in particular (+$0.83) captured a real pullback rather than just being a scratch.
3. **The two QQQ shorts and the PLTR short/long pair show a pattern of re-entering the same name shortly after a trade closes** — 4 of the 8 trades today were "second attempts" on a ticker already traded once in the session, and 2 of those 4 were losers.
4. **A net positive day (+$1.63) driven almost entirely by one strong trade (DELL long)** — worth noticing that the single best trade contributed more than the combined total of every other trade today.
5. **Next session:** keep the MU rotation going since it produced clean, confirmed setups, and continue tightening the rule against re-entering a name a second time in the same session without a genuinely new signal — especially fading a stock that's already shown strong directional strength that day.
